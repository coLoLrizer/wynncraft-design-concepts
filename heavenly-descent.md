## Major ID — Heavenly Impact  
Casting **Charge + Shift** with at least **20% Overhealth** transforms the dash into a *vertical slam*.

The player rises into the air for **1.5 seconds**, then falls along a slight arc in the direction they are looking  
(looking downward causes a direct drop; looking forward propels the player slightly ahead).

Upon landing, the ability consumes **up to 30% Overhealth and 5% Max HP**,  
dealing **AoE damage equal to 500% of the consumed HP** within a **5-block radius**.

**Damage Conversion:**  
- 20% Neutral  
- 30% Fire  
- 50% Earth
