# Telekinetic — Class Lore & Overview
*A custom Wynncraft-style class concept by Me*

---

## 1. Class Overview

**Class Name:** Telekinetic  
**Role:** Hybrid Frontline Caster  
**Weapon:** **Focus** (psionic conduit)

### Kinetic Pulse — Main Attack  
The Telekinetic does not swing a weapon. Every basic attack is a focused mental blast.

- **Shape:** Narrow cone  
- **Range:** ~3.5 blocks  
- **Damage:** Standard main-attack DPS  
- **Effect:** Mild knockback, stylized as compressed air pressure  

The class stays between melee and caster: short-range attacks, but high precision and burst potential.

---

## 2. Class Fantasy

The **Telekinetic** is a mental fighter who reshapes the battlefield through willpower alone. Air compresses, stone shatters, gravity bends — every motion is echoed by invisible force.

Gameplay revolves around **space control**, rhythm, and positional mastery. Instead of blades, you fight with **momentum, pressure, and direction**.

### Core Abilities
- **Orbital Strike** — summon orbiting stones or elemental cores that attack or collapse on command.  
- **Telekinesis** — a directional lunge used for repositioning, elevation, or isolating enemies.  
- **Magnetism** — expanding or imploding force waves that drag, scatter, or overload targets.  
- **Barrier** — a delayed psionic shield that converts pressure into survivability or counterforce.

### Archetype Themes
- **Defender** — weight, inertia, monoliths, battlefield shaping, crushing burst windows.  
- **Elementalist** — fluid combos, Air/Water/Fire variants, reactive chain effects and soft control.  
- **Mindbreaker** — speed, chained micro-explosions, psionic overload states and rapid triggers.

The Telekinetic plays as a **hybrid frontline control mage**, always adjusting distance and turning built-up pressure into decisive bursts.

## 3. Initial Lore – *Origin of the Telekinetic*

Hidden beneath the **Canyon of the Lost**, beyond landslides and collapsed temples, lie forgotten sanctums built centuries before the fall of the Silent Expanse. These chambers were once home to monks who studied not magic — but **pressure** itself.

They meditated in darkness, feeling the tremors of living stone and the weight of mountains shifting above them. Through discipline, they learned to lift boulders with thought alone, sense fractures before they surfaced, and bend gravity like a limb.

When the ancient elemental sanctums collapsed and were swallowed by the canyon, the order remained underground. Over generations, their senses sharpened; they adapted to the darkness and to the ley-lines threading through Gavel’s bedrock.

But something changed.

The **Qira Hive** began to pulse with distortions — vibrations that rippled through the canyon’s tectonic veins. The monks felt this psychic pressure immediately. Drawn by instability, they emerged to the surface for the first time in centuries.

They call themselves **Telekinetics**.

Their power is not borrowed from elements or scripture.  
It is **will made manifest — the world reshaped through thought alone.**

## 4. Archetype Lore

### 🟤 Defender — *Keepers of the Deep Stone*
Before the canyon collapsed, a sect of monks chose to remain deep underground, guarding titanic remnants of forgotten stone guardians — the same forces that once birthed the **Canyon Colossus**.

To Defenders, weight is emotion and force is language.  
A boulder is not an object — it is momentum waiting to be directed.

They tear monoliths from the earth, hurl slabs like meteors, and give every stone “memory” of motion. They sculpt the battlefield as if it were clay under their will.

**To fight a Defender is to fight the canyon itself.**

---

### 🟦 Elementalist — *Students of the Bantisu Flow*
High above the ravines, in the hidden schools of the **Bantisu Air Temple**, monks refined an art of motion: breath, posture, elemental awareness, all woven into a single flowing discipline.

Telekinetics who trained here learned that:
- air carries motion,
- water carries calm and pressure,
- fire carries intent and ignition.

Through their Focus, they shape invisible elemental cores that shift their abilities:
- **Air** creates lifts and cutting force waves  
- **Water** crushes with pressure and dampens motion  
- **Fire** ignites shockwaves and volatile bursts  

Where Defenders bend weight, Elementalists bend motion itself.

---

### 🟥 Mindbreaker — *Echoes of the Hive*
Near the borders of the **Qira Hive**, the air vibrates with psionic noise — distortions most people never consciously feel. Telekinetics feel them too strongly.

Some monks meditated too close and their thoughts fractured into overwhelming clarity. They began perceiving acceleration, ley-line “ticks,” and energy spikes long before they occurred.

Through discipline instead of madness, they learned to channel this overload.

Mindbreakers weaponize **psionic resonance**:
- accelerated Orbitals  
- rapid Magnetism triggers  
- chained micro-explosions  
- and states like **Charged**, where every input becomes a directed bolt of thought

To face a Mindbreaker is to stand inside a storm of pure intent.
